The initial idea was a quick questionnaire of random life scenarios (for now it's only 5). The answers are choices that each reflect a different instinct. Based on what the user picks, they're placed in a category at the end, with a short explanation of why they landed there.

I wanted to use life scenarios rather than just workplace problems because risk culture shows up in the instinct people reach for first, not in what they say in a workplace environment. Difficult, random situations are more personal, so people answer more honestly than they would to a "correct answer" workplace question.

But doing it this way, I feel it could be used for any other site out there, and it's not very personalized for RCB at the moment. I think that's my main problem here, though I'm sure there are more, and that's just the one I see as being prioritized.
